## Context

What problem are we solving? Which forces and constraints apply (requirements, team size,
cost, deadlines, interview defensibility)? What would happen if we did nothing?

## Decision

What we will do, stated in one or two sentences, followed by the details that matter.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier or safer.

### Negative

- What becomes harder, what we give up, and the risks we accept.

### Follow-ups

- Work this decision creates, and the signal that would make us revisit it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A:** what it offers, and why we did not choose it.
- **Option B:** what it offers, and why we did not choose it.
