# São Brás de Alportel

## Overview

São Brás de Alportel is a town and municipality in Portugal's Algarve region.

## Understand

São Brás de Alportel (SUHNW BRAHZH d(ih) ahl-poor-TEHL, /ˈsɐ̃w ˈbɾaʒ dɨ aɫ.puɾ.ˈtɛɫ/) municipality has 11,266 residents (2021 preliminary), with 4,700 living in the town. It's one of three landlocked municipalities in the Algarve.

- **Ponto de Informação Turística Municipal**

## Get in

- **Estação Autocarros** (Terminal Rodoviário, bus station) — Eva Transportes provides regional bus service.
- The nearest airport is in Faro (FAO).
- The nearest motorway is the A22 (Via do Infante de Sagres). The N2 national road provides access from there into town. The ER270 (a/k/a N270) regional road also serves the town.

## See

- **Eco Cork Factory Tour** (Eco-Fábrica de Cortiça) — Learn about the lifecycle of the cork oak tree and the process of preparing and packing the product at a cork factory. Visit a shop selling cork fashion and other items. (price: Adults €14.50, kids €7.25; hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 09:00–13:00, closed Sun)
- **Igreja Matriz de São Brás de Alportel** — 16th-century parish church with Tuscan columns and Neoclassical altarpieces, with some Rococo touches and 18th-century images.
- **Moinho do Bengado** — Historical Mediterranean-style windmill built in 1850 on a site offering spectacular views. (hours: Wed 09:30–12:30, Thu 14:00–17:00)
- **Museu do Traje** (Costume Museum) — This museum displays historical Algarvian regional clothing and way of life. (price: €2.50, kids 12 & under free; hours: Mon-Fri 10:00–13:00 & 14:00–17:00, Sat Sun holidays 14:00–17:00)

## Sleep

- **Bem Bom B&B** — Nine-room bed and breakfast run by a Belgian couple with outdoor swimming pool, fitness centre, billiards, free Wi-Fi, and free parking. (price: €88-195)
- **Casal da Eira B&B** — Eight-room rustic bed and breakfast with gardens, Mediterranean decor, swimming pool, free Wi-Fi, and free parking. (price: €68-138)
- **Farmhouse of the Palms** — Seven-room hilltop bed and breakfast in a 300-year-old farmhouse with panoramic views, outdoor swimming pool, in-room private terraces, minimalistic decor, free Wi-Fi, and free parking. (price: €133-830)
- **Panoramic Paradise B&B** — Three-room bed and breakfast with outdoor swimming pool, garden, sun terrace, and free Wi-Fi. (price: €60-129)

## Connect
