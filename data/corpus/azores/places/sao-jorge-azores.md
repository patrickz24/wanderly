# São Jorge (Azores)

## Overview

São Jorge is an island in Portugal's Azores (Açores) archipelago.

## Understand

São Jorge (SUHNW ZHOHR-zh(ih), /ˈsɐ̃w ˈʒoɾ.ʒɨ/) has two main settlements:

- **Calheta** – the eastern part of the island
- **Velas** – the western part of the island

## Get in

### By plane

- **Aérodromo de São Jorge** (SJZ) — SATA Air Açores flies from Ponta Delgada (PDL) and Terceira (TER).

### By boat

- **Atlânticoline** — Ferries from Faial, Pico, Terceira, and Graciosa.

## See

- **Igreja de Santa Bárbara** — Church built in 1770 over the ruins of an earlier church from 1485.

## Eat

- **O Branquinho** (price: €7-15; hours: Tue–Sun 11:00–14:00 & 18:30–21:30, closed Mon)
- **Restaurante Castelinho** (price: €4-21; hours: Wed–Mon 09:00–15:00 & 18:00–22:00, closed Tue)
- **Fornos de Lava** — Traditional cooking in a wood-burning oven using local ingredients. Views of Pico and Faial Islands. (price: €15-40; hours: Thu–Tue 19:00–23:00, closed Wed)

## Sleep

- **Cantinho das Buganvilias Resort** — Four-star, 19-room apartment-style hotel. Rooms have full kitchenettes, dining areas, patios with island views, and living rooms with flat-screen TVs. (price: €69-100)
- **Quinta de São Pedro** — Rural accommodation with swimming pool, free breakfast, free Wi-Fi, and free parking.
- **Hotel São Jorge Garden** — Three-star, 58-room hotel with outdoor swimming pool, free breakfast, free Wi-Fi, and free parking. (price: €54-96)
