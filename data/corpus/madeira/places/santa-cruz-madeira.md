# Santa Cruz (Madeira)

## Overview

Santa Cruz is a city on the island of Madeira, Portugal.

## Understand

Santa Cruz (SUHN-tuh KROOSH, /ˈsɐ̃.tɐ ˈkɾuʃ/) municipality has 43,005 residents (2011).

### Visitor information

- **Posto de Turismo** (Tourist Information Office) (hours: 09:00–21:30 daily)

## Get in

- **Cristiano Ronaldo International Airport** (Madeira Airport, Funchal Airport, FNC) — Although located in Santa Cruz municipality, Madeira Airport is associated with Funchal.

## See

- **Igreja de São Salvador** — 16th-century Gothic and Manueline-style church. It is similar to Funchal Cathedral, at a smaller scale, and is Madeira's 2nd-largest church. (hours: Tue–Sat 08:00–18:00, Sun 07:00–18:00, closed Mon)

## Do

- **Aquaparque Madeira** — Water park. (price: Adults €10, kids 5–12 €7, kids under 5 free; hours: 10:00–18:00 daily)

## Eat

- **Avó Micas** (price: €15-46; hours: Daily 08:00–10:00 & 11:00–18:00 & 19:00–21:00)
- **Hibiscus Restaurant** — Elegant restaurant with ocean views offering Mediterranean and local cuisine. (price: €10-35; hours: Daily 12:00–15:00 & 18:30–21:30)
- **Taberna do Petisco** (price: €8-12; hours: Tue–Sun 07:00–22:00, closed Mon)

## Sleep

- **Quinta das Colmeias** — Two three-star holiday homes. The main house sleeps 8 and the cottage sleeps 6. They share a swimming pool. A few minutes drive from Santo da Serra Golf Course and Horse Riding Centre. (price: €110-178)
- **Valle Paraizo** (Casas Valleparaizo) — Three-star, 20-room lodging in nine mountain cottages and a main house scattered around a beautiful large sloping garden. Quiet surroundings, a 20-minute drive from Funchal. (price: €68-113)
- **Vila Galé Santa Cruz** — Four-star, 262-room room hotel with sea vista. (price: €86-152)
